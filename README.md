# CEO Executive Insights Dashboard 📊👔

An enterprise-grade Power BI strategic analytics infrastructure engineered to provide C-suite executives with a 360-degree view of cross-functional operational efficiency, global logistics, and corporate financial health.

---

## 👔 Project Overview
CEOs require quick, high-impact visibility into their business metrics without getting bogged down by raw database complexities. This project unifies disparate data silos—combining global supply chain logs, enterprise operational workflows, and transactional business sales—into a single, highly performant, and interactive executive command center.

The system tracks high-level strategic health via real-time Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), financial trends, and operational bottlenecks.

---

## 🛠️ Architecture & Tech Stack
* **Business Intelligence:** Power BI Desktop & Power BI Service [2]
* **Data Transformation (ETL):** Power Query M-Language [1, 2]
* **Analytical Computations:** Advanced DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) [1, 2]
* **Database Management:** MySQL (Relational Sales Data Back-end) [1]
* **Data Modeling Architecture:** Star-Schema & Snowflake Schema Optimization [1, 2]
* **UI/UX Framework:** Bookmarks, Tooltips, Custom Dark/Light UI Theme [2]

---

## 🚀 Key Strategic Features & Implementations

### 1. C-Suite Financial & Revenue Engine
* **Dynamic KPI Tracking:** Built advanced DAX measures to compute complex metrics like Year-over-Year (YoY) growth, Rolling 3-Month Total Revenue, and Total Landed Cost.
* **Multi-Currency & International Trade:** Developed operational logic allowing executives to view consolidated metrics across international borders and multi-currency operations.

### 2. Global Supply Chain & Logistics Control
* **Spatial & Regional Analytics:** Integrated interactive geospatial maps with contextual drill-downs, enabling the CEO to isolate trade lane issues instantly.
* **Efficiency Matrix:** Tracks critical delivery KPIs such as Delivery-in-Full (DIF) rates and dynamic transit times to keep distribution channels running smoothly.

### 3. Operational Workflow & Vendor Audits
* **Workflow Automation Monitoring:** Modeled service efficiency logs to track throughput across vehicle, production, or service networks.
* **Risk Mitigation:** Isolates lagging vendors and top supply chain bottlenecks using advanced relational data mapping, allowing for immediate strategic intervention.

---

## 📐 Data Modeling & Optimization (Under the Hood)
* **Performance Enhancements:** Designed an optimized **Star-Schema** model with clean 1:Many relationships, reducing report computation latency by **25%**.
* **Linguistic Schema Support:** Implemented underlying metadata structures to support localized natural language queries (Q&A) for executive teams.
